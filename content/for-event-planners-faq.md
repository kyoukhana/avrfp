## Questions
### Is it really free to post an AV RFP?
Yes. Event planners can create an account and post AV RFPs on AVRFP at no cost. There is no fee to post an RFP and no commission charged on business awarded through the platform.

### What types of events can I post?
AVRFP can be used for meetings, conferences, conventions, trade shows, corporate events, galas, association events, exhibitions, and other events requiring audiovisual services.

### Who can view my RFP?
Your RFP is made available to AV suppliers using AVRFP to find opportunities. Sensitive documents can remain restricted until access is granted.

### Do you charge a commission?
No. AVRFP does not charge event planners or AV suppliers a commission based on the value of business awarded.

### How do I communicate with AV suppliers?
Questions and answers related to your RFP are managed through AVRFP, creating one organized place for communication.

### How do I choose an AV supplier?
You decide. Receive and review supplier proposals using your own evaluation criteria, including technical approach, experience, pricing, service, and sustainability.

### Does AVRFP choose the AV supplier for me?
No. AVRFP provides the marketplace and tools. The event planner remains responsible for evaluating proposals and selecting a supplier.
