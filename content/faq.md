## Questions
### How do I create an account?
Creating an account is easy. Click Sign Up, complete the registration form, and verify your email. Your account is free to create, and it gives you access to browse available AV RFP opportunities.

### How much does it cost to access an RFP?
Browsing opportunities is free. When you are ready to access the full RFP details and supporting documents, there is a per-RFP fee. Pricing is shown clearly before purchase.

### How do I find relevant RFP opportunities?
Browse and search RFPs by location, event type, date, or keyword to find opportunities matching your expertise and availability.

### What information will I see before purchasing an RFP?
You can view key details such as event type, location, dates, a summary of AV requirements, and important notes. Full RFP documents are available after payment.

### How do I submit my proposal?
After purchasing access, upload your proposal and requested documents directly through the platform before the submission deadline.

### Can I ask questions about an RFP?
Yes. Questions and answers are managed through the platform so suppliers participating in the opportunity receive the same information.

### Do you charge a commission?
No. AVRFP does not charge suppliers a commission on business awarded. You only pay the per-RFP fee to access specific opportunities.

### Does AVRFP guarantee business?
AVRFP connects AV suppliers with event planners. We make it easy to find and respond to opportunities, but do not guarantee an award.
