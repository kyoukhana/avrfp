## Story
### Built by People Who Understand Events.
AVRFP was founded by industry professionals who understand the time, effort, and complexity involved in sourcing AV services for meetings and events. We saw an opportunity to create a better way — a dedicated platform designed specifically for the audiovisual needs of the events industry.

Our goal is simple: to bring greater efficiency, transparency, and opportunity to the way event planners and AV suppliers work together.
