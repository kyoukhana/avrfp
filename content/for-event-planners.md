## FAQs
### Is it really free to post an AV RFP?
Yes. Event planners can create an account and post an AV RFP without a posting fee.

### Who can view my RFP?
Your RFP is shared with qualified AV suppliers using the platform.

### How do I communicate with AV suppliers?
Questions and answers stay organized through the platform for easy reference.

### What types of events can I post?
You can post RFPs for conferences, galas, meetings, live events, and other professional productions.

### Do you charge a commission?
No commission is charged on business awarded through your RFP.

### How do I choose the right AV supplier?
Review each proposal using your own requirements and selection criteria.
